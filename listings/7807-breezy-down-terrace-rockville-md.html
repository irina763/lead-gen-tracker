The location

From above: the home, the street, and the parkland and amenities that surround it. Tap any image to enlarge.

7807 Breezy Down Terrace sits on a quiet terrace at the edge of Rock Creek Regional Park — a close-in Rockville address where an established, green setting still comes with real connectivity. Shady Grove Metro on the Red Line is under a mile away, with the ICC, I-270, and Route 370 only minutes out.

A residents' pool, Blueberry Hill Park, Lake Needwood, Needwood Golf Course, and King Farm's shops and dining are all within easy reach — the kind of location people pay a premium for a few blocks over.
